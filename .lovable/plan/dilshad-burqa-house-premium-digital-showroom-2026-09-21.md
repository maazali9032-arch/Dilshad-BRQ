# Dilshad Burqa House — Premium Digital Showroom

## Goal
Create a polished, single-page fashion catalogue that makes visitors want to experience Dilshad Burqa House in person. The site will use the supplied storefront photograph as its dominant visual and will not include any e-commerce features.

## Experience
- Full-screen cinematic opening with the storefront image, restrained overlay, editorial serif typography, sequential reveals, and clear collection/visit actions.
- Compact sticky navigation that transforms on scroll, plus an elegant full-screen mobile menu.
- Editorial brand statement followed by asymmetric Abayas, Burqas, and Hijabs presentation without invented products, prices, or availability.
- Immersive showroom gallery using the supplied photograph in varied crops, with subtle zoom and fullscreen viewing. Since only one image is currently attached, the layout will reuse intentional detail crops rather than inventing additional business photography.
- “Step Into Dilshad,” reasons to visit, verified Google rating, destination-style location section, dramatic final call to action, and minimal footer.
- Working phone, WhatsApp, Google Maps directions, and Google reviews links.

## Visual Direction
- Warm ivory and champagne surfaces, deep charcoal/black, and restrained burgundy/rose accents inspired by the storefront.
- High-fashion serif headlines paired with a clean contemporary sans serif.
- Generous whitespace, sharp editorial composition, restrained borders, and minimal rounding.
- Refined motion: opening reveals, masked image entrances, subtle parallax, scroll-triggered typography, hover zoom, and reduced-motion support.

## Technical Details
- Build the complete experience at `/` with reusable frontend sections and accessible interactions.
- Store the uploaded storefront photo through the project asset system and preserve its natural proportions.
- Use CSS and lightweight React motion patterns for performance; lazy-load below-the-fold image instances.
- Add a keyboard-accessible image lightbox and mobile navigation.
- Add route-specific title, description, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, canonical URL, and ClothingStore structured data using only supplied facts.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, lightbox, links, and runtime console state.
